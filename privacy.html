Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

BOTC ("the App") is an unofficial fan-made companion tool for the tabletop game Blood on the Clocktower. This policy explains what information the App handles and why. The App is designed to be played with a small group of people you already know, and it collects as little personal information as possible.

Information the App uses

  • Camera. With your permission, the App uses your device camera only to scan a game's QR code so you can join a session. Camera images are processed on your device and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Display name and (optional) profile photo. If you choose to set a name or photo, these are shared with the other players in your game so they can recognize you. A profile photo is provided by you; you can skip it.
  • Gameplay data. Your role, actions, votes, and other in-game state are synced among the devices in your game so the game can run. This data exists only to operate the current game.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the App may register a device push token so it can alert you to game events (for example, when it is your turn). The token identifies your device for delivery and is not used to track you.

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your email address, phone number, or contacts.

How information is stored and shared

  • Game data is transmitted and temporarily stored using Supabase, a third-party hosting service, so that the devices in a game can stay in sync. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Push notifications are delivered through the Expo push service and the platform notification services (Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging). See the Expo Privacy Policy.
  • Gameplay data is retained only as long as needed to run games and is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

What the App does not do

  • No advertising, and no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of your information for marketing.
  • No location tracking.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
